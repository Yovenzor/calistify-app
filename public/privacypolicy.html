CALISTIFY — Health Data Privacy Policy

What we read

With your permission, CALISTIFY reads only:

  • Steps (daily totals shown in the app)
  • Active energy burned (shown in the app)
  • Workout / exercise sessions (counted in the app)

We request access only to these types. We never request access to anything we do not use.

What we write

When you finish and save a workout in CALISTIFY, that workout session (its start time, end time and duration) is saved to Apple Health or Health Connect so your training history stays in one place. We do not fabricate or estimate health values such as calories.

Where health data goes

Health data is read on your device and displayed to you. It is not uploaded, sold, or shared with third parties. The only workout details stored in your CALISTIFY account are the workouts you log in the app itself.

Your control

You can change or revoke access at any time, without contacting us:

  • iPhone: Health → Profile → Apps → CALISTIFY
  • Android: Health Connect → App permissions → CALISTIFY

Revoking access stops all reading and writing immediately, and the app updates its connection status the next time you open it.
